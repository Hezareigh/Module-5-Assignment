Data analysis suggests that less than half (41%) of the people who were given a bar coupon accepted the coupon. However, of the people who accepted the coupon, majority are those who go to a bar at most once a month. Therefore, the coupon is likely effective for bringing in customers who don't go to bars often. Also, the data suggests that people with lower income are more likely to accept the coupon. However, those with lower income could be making up the majority of those don't go to a bar frequently.

As next steps it is useful to investigate the distance of the driver to the bar and how that affected the acceptance rate.

Also, generally it is expected that people don't go to a bar in the morning and that is something that can be verified if more people accepted the bar coupon in the afternoon or evening compared to morning, or did the coupon bring in customers when the bar is not at it's busiest.
